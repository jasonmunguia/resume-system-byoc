# The HHH qualification skeleton

The Business Analyst Intern slide is **canonical**. It is not a sample output to
be improved on — it is the worked example from which the pattern is read. Never
rewrite it. Pattern-match new roles against it.

## The canonical slide

> **Qualifications for a Business Analyst Intern**
> - Degree
> - Basic knowledge of (Tableau, Power BI, communicate and influence, Microsoft
>   Windows and Office, including Word, PowerPoint, Advanced Excel, and Outlook),
>   Deadlines, and organizational skills.
> - Ability to work with others
> - Takes direction and criticism

## What the structure encodes

Four bullets, and only one of them is about the job.

| # | Bucket | Function | Varies by role? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Credential** — Degree | Threshold check | **No** |
| 2 | **Tool stack + core ability + self-management** | The actual role | **Yes — the only bucket that swaps** |
| 3 | **Collaboration** — Ability to work with others | Risk removal | **No** |
| 4 | **Coachability** — Takes direction and criticism | Risk removal | **No** |

**Three of four bullets are risk removal.** For an intern requisition the hiring
manager is not asking *is this person exceptional* — they are asking *will this
be a bad hire*. HHH's own framing (p.3): a manager would rather make no hire than
a bad hire, and the process restarts rather than settling. So the qualification
list is mostly insurance, and exactly one bullet is capability.

Bucket 2 is itself a compound of three things fused into one sentence:
- **named software** (Tableau, Power BI, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook)
- **one core human ability** (*communicate and influence*)
- **self-management** (*deadlines, organizational skills*)

## Deriving a new role

Hold 1, 3, and 4 fixed. Swap only bucket 2 — the tool stack and the core ability
that names the job.

**Product Manager Intern**
> - Degree
> - Basic knowledge of (Jira, roadmapping, SQL, analytics dashboards, Word,
>   PowerPoint, Excel), **prioritize and influence without authority**, deadlines,
>   and organizational skills.
> - Ability to work with others
> - Takes direction and criticism

**Business Development / GTM Intern**
> - Degree
> - Basic knowledge of (CRM, prospecting tools, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook),
>   **communicate value and handle objections**, deadlines, and organizational
>   skills.
> - Ability to work with others
> - Takes direction and criticism

The core ability is the one phrase carrying the role:
*communicate and influence* (BA) → *prioritize and influence without authority*
(PM) → *communicate value and handle objections* (GTM).

## Why this matters for bullet assignment

The skeleton says which qualifications are **table stakes** and which are
**differentiating**, and they are spent differently on the page.

- Buckets 1, 3, 4 (degree, teamwork, coachability) are table stakes. They still
  need proof — an unevidenced claim gets discounted — but one bullet each is
  enough, and they should never consume a premium slot.
- Bucket 2 is where the resume competes.

This also explains a measured gap that looks strange otherwise: **Coachability**
scores low in every corpus (demand 2–4) because JDs rarely *write it down* — yet
HHH gives it a full quarter of the canonical list. Low measured demand, high
real weight. When the skeleton and the corpus disagree about a risk-removal
bucket, the skeleton wins: JDs under-state what everyone assumes.

That is the general rule for reconciling the two sources — **measurement ranks
capability; the skeleton ranks risk.** Neither alone gives the right bullet
allocation.
